Our Core Philosophy

Change for the better

Parivartan in Sanskrit translates to change; a change for the better. Representing change in a visual manner was quite the challenge.

Epitome

The leaf came about as an epitome of showing change; its cycle being that of birth, growth, fall and regeneration.

Team Effort
