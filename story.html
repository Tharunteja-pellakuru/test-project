Our Name

What Parivartan
Means to Us

In Sanskrit, Parivartan means transformation. It became the foundation of how we think, build, and partner with every client.

Change for Better

Parivartan in Sanskrit translates to change — a change for the better. Representing change in a visual manner was our founding challenge, and we answered with a leaf that embodies growth.

Growth & Evolution

The leaf came about as an epitome of showing change — its cycle being that of birth, growth, fall, and regeneration. Every client engagement is a cycle of continuous improvement.

Collaborative Innovation

It is this change that is constant but not quite. An inspired thinking is always a team effort — the most important constituent of the team being the client themselves.
