What services does eParivartan offer? +
We provide end-to-end digital services including website design and development, mobile app development (Android & iOS), e-commerce store setup, backend API development, UI/UX design, SEO, and digital marketing strategy.
Do you work with small businesses and startups? +
Absolutely. We work with businesses of all sizes — from solo founders launching their first product to established companies looking to modernise their digital presence. Every project gets the same level of care and professionalism.
How long does it take to build a website? +
A standard business website typically takes 3–6 weeks from kickoff to launch. More complex projects like custom web applications or e-commerce platforms can take 8–16 weeks. We always share a clear timeline at the start of your project.
Will my website be SEO-friendly? +
Yes — SEO best practices are baked into every website we build. This includes proper heading structure, fast load times, mobile responsiveness, schema markup, clean URLs, and meta tag optimisation. We treat SEO as a foundation, not an afterthought.
Can you redesign my existing website? +
Yes, and it's one of our most common projects. We audit your existing site, identify conversion issues and UX problems, then redesign and rebuild it with a clear focus on improving engagement, reducing bounce rate, and driving more enquiries.
How is eParivartan different from other agencies? +
24 years in the industry means we've seen what works and what doesn't. We combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution — no outsourcing, no templates, no cookie-cutter solutions. You get a dedicated team that treats your project like it's our own business on the line.
